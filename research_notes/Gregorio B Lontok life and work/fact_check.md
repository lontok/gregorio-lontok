# Fact check of "Gregorio B Lontok life and work"

Checked on 2026-09-29 by re-opening each cited source. Archive.org items were checked against the full djvu text. Google Books items were checked with the search-inside endpoint (books?id=X&jscmd=SearchWithinVolume2&q=...), which returns the same snippets as the q= page. The report itself was not edited.

| Home listed as Rosario, Batangas (UP Commencement 1920) | verified | "Grogoiio B. Lontac, Rosario, Batangas" (OCR). Ambrosio Lontok y Mendoza, Lobo, is on the same list. |

| Class of 1923, "Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (Supplementary)" (PA vol. XI djvu) | verified | Heading "CLASS OF 1923" then "Bachelor of Science in Agriculture (Supplementary)", with "Lontok, Gregorio B. See Class of 1920, Bachelor of Agriculture, supra." |
