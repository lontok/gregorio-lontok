Gregorio B. Lontok

Lolo, agronomist of the Negros cane fields, 1894 to 1959

He grew up in Batangas, studied agriculture at Los Baños, and spent the late 1920s running sugarcane trials on the haciendas of Negros. He ended his career as Superintendent of La Granja Experiment Station in La Carlota. This page tests the family's own account of his life against old journals, directories, and indexes, and it links every claim to its source.

How to read the labels

  • ConfirmedA source outside the family papers says it.
  • PartialA source supports part of it or fits it closely.
  • Family onlySo far it appears only in the family's own documents.

Family only doesn't mean wrong. It means nobody has found the paper trail yet.

How this was researched: in September 2026, research agents searched digitized journals and directories on Internet Archive and Google Books, plus genealogy indexes, with Firecrawl and Tavily. They also searched the Consensus and Elicit scholarly databases. Those two had no records of him, which is expected for 1920s trade-journal work, but they supplied the scientific background on mosaic disease, P.O.J. 2878, and cassava. A separate pass then re-opened the key sources to check the quotations and labels.
